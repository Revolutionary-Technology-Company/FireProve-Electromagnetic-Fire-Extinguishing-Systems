## 1. Structural Bottom Mount and Internals Layout (src/fireprove_internal_chassis.scad)
The bottom mount functions as the high-load mechanical anchor that bolts the turret to structural concrete decks or steel vehicle plates. Inside the chassis, the 16-state analog logic boards, the high-power Class-D amplifier, and the dual-axis gimbals are nested to protect them from high-vibration stress and heat creep.

## 2. Internal Mechanical Layout Specifications
To survive the combination of external heat fields and high-amplitude internal acoustic vibrations, the internal mechanical configuration adheres to the following layout constraints:

* Continuous-Rotation Slip Ring: A gold-on-gold alloy contact capsule slip ring sits directly inside the central 45 mm core conduit axis. This enables continuous 360° pan movement while routing thick high-current power rails from the Class-D amplifier up to the moving titanium driver, preventing cable fatigue and twisting.
* Vibration Isolation Standoffs: The custom 8-layer Micro-ATX motherboard is mounted on shock-dampening neoprene isolation standoffs. This absorbs high-frequency vibrations from the dual-axis gimbals and the acoustic blast, preventing micro-fractures on surface-mount capacitors and BGA processing pins.
* Star-Grounding Bus Bar: To protect the discrete 0.0V–1.0V analog logic intervals from switching noise generated by the adjacent GaN power stage and the ASUS TUF RTX 50 GPU, a solid copper ground bus bar is bolted directly to the titanium base frame. All shielding layers and return lines converge at this single star-ground point to prevent ground loop voltage skew.

## 3. Integrated Shock and Vibration Safety Loop
High-amplitude low-frequency acoustic sound waves exert physical back-pressure inside the turret housing. To monitor structural stabilization metrics, a tri-axial accelerometer is tracked inside src/safety_interlock.py.
If a mount bolt loosens or a mechanical resonance spike threatens internal board tracks, the firmware drops power via the safety bus:

# Added structural vibration check loop within src/safety_interlock.pydef evaluate_structural_g_force_resonance(self, live_accelerometer_g_forces):
    """
    Monitors structural vibration resonance thresholds inside the component chassis.
    Trips an immediate hardware shutdown if mechanical resonance spikes.
    """
    # Max allowable mechanical stress threshold set to 4.5 Gs of structural acceleration
    max_allowable_vibration_g = 4.5
    
    x_g, y_g, z_g = live_accelerometer_g_forces
    total_resonance_vector = math.sqrt(x_g**2 + y_g**2 + z_g**2)
    
    if total_resonance_vector >= max_allowable_vibration_g:
        print(f"[CRITICAL FAULT] Structural resonance threshold breached: {total_resonance_vector:.2f} Gs!")
        self.hardware_permissive = False
        return "RESONANCE_HALT_REQUIRED"
        
    return "CHASSIS_STABLE"

If the structural vibration vector breaches the safety limit, the system instantly grounds out the amplifier circuits and flags a component vibration error to the Edwards FireWorks Workstation, ensuring facilities crews can check mounting torques before the turret resumes active operation.
